# Cybersecurity GRC Portfolio
Greg Campbell's portfolio of his work for Cybersecurity GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance).  These documents are for a fictional small biochem/biotech company of about 50 on-site employees going through a transition from its old brand (Sample Company) to its new brand (Oscorp).  I acted as an outside consultant to assess and, as prudent, help this organization improve its cybersecurity posture via improving its internal policies and procedures, as well as its compliance with NIST standards.

To download a document, click its name then the down arrow icon for Download raw file.

Documents here were adapted from the SANS Institute and GRC Mastery.

The files here include:

- Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

- Access Management Policy

- Cyber Incident Response Plan Template

- ISMS (Information Security Management System) Meeting Minutes Template

- ISMS (Information Security Management System) Nonconformity Log Template

- ISO 27001 Assessment Spreadsheet with Mandatory and Annex A Controls

- Password Policy

- TPRM (Third Party Risk Management) Questionnaire
